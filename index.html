Mutton Biryani مٹن بریانی

Long-grain basmati layered with tender bone-in mutton, fried onions, potato and a proper Karachi-style kick of spice. Named in more than 220 reviews.

Nihari

Beef shank slow-cooked overnight in a silky, spiced gravy. Finished with ginger, green chilli and lemon.
